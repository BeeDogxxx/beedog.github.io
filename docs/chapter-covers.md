Eight section entrances extend the existing ONE MORE comic direction. Three primary pages get original full-bleed art; the other five use shorter typographic openers. Illustrations are symbolic editorial scenes, not documentary depictions of the author's projects or travels.

The large English titles are decorative live HTML text in the existing local display font. Chinese H1 headings remain the accessible page titles. Artwork is noninteractive and marked decorative. All text remains readable without image loading. On mobile, the image focuses toward the subject and the lettering sits over a stronger bottom gradient. No new character animation is included.

Implementation: `css/comic-details.css`, existing page headers and versioned shared references. Game and article reading layouts retain their existing purpose. Portfolio's ACE case diagram remains intact.
